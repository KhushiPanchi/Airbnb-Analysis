# Airbnb Analysis | Power Bi Project
## Project Overview
This project presents an interactive Airbnb Listings & Reviews Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI and the Airbnb dataset provided by Maven Analytics.
The dashboard is designed to provide a clear overview of Airbnb's marketplace performance ,ratings and reviews and help users explore factors such as listing prices, property types, availability, and guest satisfaction.
## Dataset Used
-<a href=https://mavenanalytics.io/data-playground/airbnb-listings-reviews >Dataset</a>
## Tech Stack
List of the key technologies used to build the dashboard.
-	Power Bi Desktop: Main data visualization platform used for report creation.
-	Power Query: Data transformation and cleaning layer for reshaping and preparing the data.
-	DAX: Used for calculated measures, dynamic visuals and conditional logic.
-	Data Modeling: Relationships established among tables.
-	File Format: .pbix for development and .png for dashboard preview.
## Business Objective
The analysis focuses on:
### Overview
-	How big the Airbnb presence across major global cities?
-	How has the Airbnb grown over time and where did it peak?
-	Which city contribute most to the Airbnb’s overall business?
-	Are listings concentrated or evenly spread across cities?
### Market share by city
-	Which city dominates Airbnb listings globally?
-	Are a few cities driving most of the supply?
-	How much of the total market do top cities control?
-	What is the comulative contribution of cities like Paris ,NYC and Sydney?
-	Is Airbnb supply fragmented or highly concentrated?
### Property Type & Pricing
-	Which property types are most commonly listed?
-	Are hotel rooms more expensive than Airbnb stays?
-	Why might travellers prefer Airbnb over hotels in certain cities?
-	Does pricing explain higher adoption in cities like Paris?
### Ratings and Customer Segmentation
-	Which cities deliver the best overall guest experience?
-	Which cities are underperforming on ratings?
-	Are low ratings driven by cleanliness,value for money or communication?
-	Is high supply always equal to high quality?
### Review Frequency
-	How often do customers actually leave reviews?
-	Are reviews mostly repeat customers or one time users?
-	What percentage of users write reviews more than once?
-	Can reviews be trusted or a proxy for Engagement?
### Seasonality Analysis
-	Which cities peak during which months?
-	Is Airbnb demans seasonal or stable throughout the year?
-	Do European cities behave differently from non-European cities?
-	How does travel seasonality vary by geography?
### Trust and Host Verification
-	How trustworthy are Airbnb hosts overall?
-	What percentage of hosts are fully verified?
-	How rare are completely unverified and anonymous hosts?
-	Is Airbnb doing enough to protect customers?

-	Seasonality: It is a Ribbon chart which explains the total percentage of monthly reviews each of the top city gets.
-	Trust: It is a KPI kind of visual that compares the percentage of people on different aspects such does have Profile Pic or not and Identity verified or not.
